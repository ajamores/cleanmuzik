# T-222 — Tag + art from the accepted identity in `_accept`

> **Wave 2 of epic [`T-220`](T-220.md) · needs T-221.** Base: **ADR-033** (ratified 2026-08-26). The
> heart of the epic: change the *source* of the tags/art written on a land, not the land decision.

## Scope

In `app/import_seam.py`'s land tail (`_accept` → `_ensure_full_match`), replace the MusicBrainz-hydration
tag source with the accepted identity:

- **Shazam-backed identity** (Shazam among the ≥2 agreeing senses): build tags from the widened Shazam
  record (T-221) — artist/album/title, year/genre when present — and use `art_url` as the cover
  (downloaded in T-223), YouTube thumbnail as fallback.
- **AcoustID-only identity** (Shazam missed, AcoustID + title corroborate): **one thin
  `get_recording`** by the AcoustID recording MBID supplies tags + art — via `musicbrainzngs` directly,
  **no fan-out** (T-208 discipline), fail-soft to provisional/review, never blocking. This is ADR-033
  decision 2 (owner option 1).
- **Corroboration authorises the source:** a Shazam record is written only when Shazam is among the
  agreeing senses — never off a lone dissenting Shazam vote (ADR-021: Shazam never lands alone; preserved).

The senses gate, the 2-of-3 vote, the LLM adjudicator, and the T-219 fast-path are **not** touched — only
what `_accept` writes once an identity is accepted.

## Done when

- A Shazam-corroborated land writes tags from the Shazam record and takes its cover from `art_url` (or the
  thumbnail fallback), with **no** `_ensure_full_match` MusicBrainz hydration on that path.
- An AcoustID-only land tags from exactly one `get_recording` (asserted: one call, no per-candidate
  fan-out) and fails soft to review on a miss/error.
- Land/park outcomes are unchanged vs. today except for the accepted tag-source change; the Frank
  Ocean/Coldplay fixture still parks.
- Seam tests (`test_import_seam.py`) assert, per accepted branch, the written tags and which art tier
  supplied the cover — external behaviour, not call sequence.

## Not

- No genre from the reconcile LLM (it authors identity, not genre; ADR-033 decision 5 — genre comes from
  Shazam here).
- Do not delete beets yet (T-224); this ticket can still route through the beets item where convenient, so
  long as the *tag/art source* is the accepted identity. The writer swap is T-223.
