# T-214 — Narrate the identity stage (perceived speed)

**Status:** untriaged, **buildable anytime** — safe, non-engine, independent of any release. The design
council's near-zero-risk lever (2026-08-13); the biggest change to how a landing *feels*, at no correctness
risk.

## Problem
The identify window (`jobs.py` 341→406) is one unbroken 16–19s freeze showing only "Identifying…". Nothing
is broken, but a static freeze *reads* as broken. The stage internally runs four steps in sequence already.

## Approach (pure notification; pipeline order + outcomes byte-identical)
- Thread a **try-wrapped** progress callback through `import_song`/`choose_item` and emit sub-stage SSE at
  boundaries that already exist serially: **Fingerprinting → Matching MusicBrainz → Cross-checking Shazam →
  Reconciling**. The callback must never throw into the hot path.
- Client: show the sub-status under the existing `Identifying` stage in `TrackCard` (the STAGE model + rail
  are already there).

## Done when
The identify freeze reads as four steps that visibly move; no ordering or land/park change.

## Why it's worth more than the seconds
Independent of whether [`T-208`](T-208.md)'s actual-speed engine change is ever built, this is the single
biggest lever on what the owner perceives while a song lands. Distinct from actual-speed work
([`T-215`](T-215.md) Shazam hoist).
