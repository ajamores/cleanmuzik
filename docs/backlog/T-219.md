# T-219 — Corroboration fast-path: skip the LLM reconcile when the fingerprint already agrees

**Status:** untriaged — filed 2026-08-24 from the muziktest head-to-head. A steady-cost speed lever,
secondary to [`T-210`](T-210.md) (the MB tail is the bigger remaining cost) but additive. Continues the
T-21x speed series.

## The idea (transplanted from muziktest's Confidence gate)

cleanmuzik calls the LLM `reconcile_fn` on **every** track (~3–6s each). muziktest doesn't: its Confidence
gate takes a **corroborated fast path** — when the fingerprint's identity already agrees with the Source's
own title, it auto-verifies with **no AI call**. On the shared 14-track playlist that fast-pathed **8 of 12
matched tracks**; the LLM ran only twice.

Port the same gate to cleanmuzik: when the dominant AcoustID fingerprint's recording **and** its
artist/title corroborate the YouTube-derived title (the `source_signals`), auto-land on the fingerprint
**without** invoking the reconcile LLM. Fall through to the existing 2-of-3 reconcile gate only when the
fingerprint is weak or the title doesn't corroborate — exactly the cases the LLM exists to adjudicate.

## Why it's worth it (and its size)

- After T-208, the LLM is part of the steady ~15s non-MB floor (T-218: ~3.5s/call, rock-stable). Removing
  it from the corroborated majority is a real per-track win on the common path.
- **Not the biggest lever** — the MB *latency tail* (T-210) is #1. This is the next steady rock, not the
  boulder. Do T-210 first.

## Guardrails (do not weaken the safety spine)

- The fast-path is **additive**: it only ever *skips* the LLM on a strong+corroborated match. Every weak or
  uncorroborated case still runs the full reconcile gate (ADR — the 2-of-3 rule is the safety spine).
- Must NOT reintroduce the "trust fp when dominant" shortcut T-208's "Not" section forbids for the marquee
  cases: the fast-path fires only when the fingerprint's identity **and** the source title agree — the
  Pa Salieu case (fp dominant on the *wrong* recording, title disagrees) still falls through to the gate.
- Engine-touching (changes the land decision path) → same acceptance bar as T-208: re-run the corpus
  compare, prove land/park + tags unchanged except where a corroborated track now lands without the LLM.
