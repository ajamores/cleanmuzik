CleanMuzik crest — Rev C

Recut to the reference proportions: wide landscape badge with outward wing-tip corners, sides angling in, a curly-brace bottom meeting at a centre nub, and a 3D crown seated over the top edge — depth from darker translucent fills, not just strokes. Soundwave dropped; the crest and the words carry it. Drawn paths, no fonts, all currentColor.

Technical notes
  • Pure SVG, landscape viewBox 30 0 200 144; letters are drawn paths (10×14 glyph grid, stroked + filled), no <text>, no fonts.
  • All geometry in currentColor — set color: #3fb6d8 (dark) or #0e7d9c (light) on the container. Depth fills use fill-opacity="0.35" on currentColor so they track both themes without a second colour token.
  • The outer and inner outlines are split where the crown band overlaps the top edge, so the crown genuinely sits on the badge rather than in front of a line running through it.
