This is the "manual-resolution vertebra" made concrete: a field on the card where you supply the answer you already know.

04

When it's just not in MusicBrainz

Your re-search already asked MusicBrainz directly, in your words — so an empty result means the recording genuinely isn't in their database (normal for bootlegs, mixtape rips, and YouTube-only mixes). No dead panel: search again with different terms, or — the real last resort — keep it with your own tags.

05

Last resort — keep it with your own tags

For a track you want that no database knows. Type what you know and it lands in your library as-is — beets still writes the tags and files it, there's just no MusicBrainz match behind them. The honest trade-off is on the card: no cover art or auto-genre, because those come from a match. In your library beats in the trash, and you can re-tag it later.
