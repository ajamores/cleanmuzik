CleanMuzik / Design-panel synthesis / Ticket T-034 · four independent reviewers

Stop making the guess smarter. Make the miss cheap to fix.

A track that should have been easy — Nipsey Hussle's "All Get Right" — parked with junk candidates because a reversed YouTube title fooled the text search. The question on the table: is the fix a taller auto-match engine, or a design change? Four reviewers, four lenses, one answer.

The trade-off

Accept that the machine stays “dumb” about messy titles at acquire time → gain a review card where the present owner supplies the right answer, retiring the whole heuristic tower and covering every case it could never crack.

At acquire time you're sitting right there and a miss costs one park plus one action. So the win isn't a higher auto-match rate — it's lowering the cost of the miss. Auto-match only earns its keep in R2 migrate: unattended, thousands of files.

02

Two facts that settle it

found independently
The engine is 90% there

It can already land the correct track.

resolve_import / _forced_match take any MusicBrainz recording ID and land it — no fingerprint, no requirement it was ever a parked candidate. The only thing blocking the score-100 answer is one guard, _validate_weak_match, that restricts your choice to the five junk candidates.

The design hole

You can reject a wrong answer, not supply the right one.

The review card offers pick-a-candidate or Reject (which throws the track away). You know the answer is two words; there's no field for them. And every title the tower can't crack still lands in this same recourse-less panel — so the manual field is needed either way.

Cheap and safe, but doesn't fix the inversion. Earns its place on unattended migrate volume, not here.

BUILD NOW

The missing exit: supply the right answer instead of only rejecting wrong ones. Lands through the existing resolve path.
