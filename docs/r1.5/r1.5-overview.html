The shift

From a judge that parks mistakes to an engine that fixes them

Today, identity comes from a single acoustic fingerprint down a slow chain of lookups. When it's wrong, the only safe move is to hand the song to a human. R1.5 gathers three independent senses and reconciles them.

A — rejected

Veto-only adjudicator

The LLM could confirm the fingerprint or park it — never correct it. Safe, but no speed, and every mistag became a chore for you.

B — chosen

Multi-sense reconciliation

Three senses vote. When two agree, the engine lands the right answer itself — even when the fingerprint got it wrong. Facts still come only from a real lookup.

The pipeline · one organ changes

The safety spine · 2-of-3 vote

Three witnesses, one decision

Each sense is a witness to the song's identity. Auto-land needs at least two to agree on artist and title, and the identity must resolve to a real MusicBrainz ID. One dissenter, or no real ID, and it parks for you.

The two guardrails

What keeps it honest

Rule 1 · the vote

Senses must agree

Auto-land needs ≥2 of the three senses on the same artist + title. The LLM may override a wrong fingerprint — but only when two other senses back it. A lone sense always parks.

Rule 2 · the facts

IDs come from a real lookup

The permanent MusicBrainz ID comes from the fingerprint or the ISRC lookup — never the LLM's memory. Already how it works today; carried forward, not new. No invented IDs.

The payoff · measured on 26 real rips

The identity stage collapses

The old path is a chain of one-call-per-second lookups. B replaces it with one Shazam call, one exact ID lookup, and one LLM call. Enrichment (art, lyrics, genre) is unchanged.

The one accepted risk — eyes open

If two senses are genuinely fooled onto the same wrong song, the engine auto-lands wrong. No structure fully removes this — it's the price of B's speed and its power to fix mistakes. The override case is validated at n=1 (Pa Salieu); wider real-use sampling is owed. Recorded, not hidden.
